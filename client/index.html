SDG Port Hub: sustainability assessment for ports

SDG Port Hub implements the SDG Attributes framework, a peer-reviewed model that assesses port sustainability against the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 17) through 84 indicators in 20 thematic dimensions. The full method is described in the article cited below.
